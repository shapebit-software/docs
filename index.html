The direction

A system you can reason about.

01

Secure by design

Boot trust, encrypted data, and recovery are treated as parts of one system—not isolated features.

02

Predictable lifecycle

Updates and system state are designed to be controlled, inspectable, and recoverable.

03

Open engineering

Architecture decisions, constraints, and unfinished work are documented as the project evolves.
